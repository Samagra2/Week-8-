📊 Data Analysis Capstone Project

Overview

End-to-end business analysis using real-world datasets covering real estate pricing, customer churn, and sales performance.

Datasets Used

  • House Prices (Price Prediction)
  • Customer Churn (Retention Analysis)
  • Sales Data (Revenue Optimization)

Tech Stack

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Key Outcomes

  • Identified price-driving features in real estate
  • Detected high-risk churn customers
  • Analyzed region-wise sales performance

Business Impact

Provided data-driven recommendations leading to improved pricing, customer retention strategies, and sales optimization.
