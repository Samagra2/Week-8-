# 📊 Complete Business Analysis Capstone Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This capstone project presents an **end-to-end business analysis** using real-world datasets to derive actionable, data-driven insights. The project covers three critical business domains:

- 🏠 House Price Analysis  
- 👥 Customer Churn Analysis  
- 📈 Sales Performance Analysis  

The goal is to apply data analysis techniques, visualize insights effectively, and translate findings into **business recommendations and implementation plans**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Perform complete data analysis from raw data to insights
- Identify key business drivers and patterns
- Create professional visualizations
- Provide actionable recommendations for business decision-making
- Build a **portfolio-ready**, reproducible project

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Property area has a strong positive correlation with house price
- Customers with higher monthly charges are more likely to churn
- Churned customers, though fewer, pose significant revenue risk
- Sales performance varies significantly across regions
- Sales trends indicate possible seasonal or promotional effects

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Focus real estate marketing on high-impact pricing features
- Introduce targeted retention strategies for high-risk churn customers
- Optimize pricing plans to reduce churn
- Replicate best-performing regional sales strategies
- Monitor KPIs regularly for data-driven decision-making

---

## 🗺️ Implementation Plan
1. Identify high-risk customer segments
2. Apply targeted offers and pricing strategies
3. Optimize regional sales strategies
4. Monitor churn rate, revenue, and sales KPIs monthly
